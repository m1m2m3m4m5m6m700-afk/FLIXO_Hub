# agent-lab Roles

builder: implements bounded changes.
explorer: researches and produces evidence.
adv-builder: attacks implementation assumptions.
adv-explorer: attacks research conclusions.
steward: checks provenance, policy, and repository hygiene.
adv-steward: attacks governance and provenance.
integrator: prepares a Candidate Bundle for execution review.

No role can certify its own work. Solver and Opponent identities must satisfy independence policy.
