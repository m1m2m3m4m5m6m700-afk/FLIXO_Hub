# CALL Constraints

Status: MANDATORY

1. No objective means no work.
2. No assignment means no agent work.
3. Every assignment requires a distinct Solver and Opponent.
4. Opponent independence must be proven before Solver disclosure.
5. Every material claim requires evidence.
6. Solver cannot self-certify or self-arbitrate.
7. Candidate requires completed opposition and exact SHA binding.
8. Candidate requires Red Team review.
9. Verification must be independent of Solver, Opponent, and Red Team.
10. Learning occurs only after promotion.
11. XP requires evidence and disposition references.
12. Policy is versioned, hashed, immutable during a mission, and not negotiable by agents.
13. Policy revision or exception requires escalation and separate review.
14. CALL is orchestration only and cannot become a second executor, registry, verifier, or certification authority.
15. CALL never writes directly to main.
16. CALL never bypasses the canonical execution boundary.
17. Failed artifacts are retained with disposition and provenance.
18. Replans retire prior artifact identities and require a fresh assignment boundary.
19. Agent workspaces and credentials must be isolated.
20. Recovery is bounded by retry, loop, cost, and duration limits.
21. Production governance issue #1125 / ruleset 23854302 is a human-administration track and is not represented as fixed by code.
