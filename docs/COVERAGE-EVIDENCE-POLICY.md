# FLIXO Coverage Evidence Policy

## Canonical evidence
Coverage certification is based on an exact-SHA internal coverage artifact generated by the repository's current test/coverage workflow. The evidence must record:
- the exact checkout SHA;
- a non-empty coverage file;
- source, line-found, and line-hit records;
- a cryptographic hash for the coverage artifact.

The retired `apps/agent-editor` path is not a current coverage authority because that runtime was removed during recovery.

## External Codecov
Codecov is not a certification authority. If Codecov is part of the final candidate requirements, its result must be genuinely observed or an owner-approved repository policy must explicitly document why the internal exact-SHA artifact is the accepted gate. A missing/failed external upload is never treated as PASS.

## Fail closed
Missing, malformed, stale, or SHA-mismatched coverage evidence is a failure.
